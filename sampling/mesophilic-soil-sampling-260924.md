# Protocol

## Overview

This protocol describes the sampling of mesophilic soils.

It aims to isolate mainly spore-forming Gram-positive bacteria, but it can also be used for the isolation of other microorganisms.

## Prerequisites

- A way to determine decimal degree coordinates, altitude, and temperature in the field.
- A metal spatula or similar.

## Consumables

- A 50 mL screw-cap tube

## Steps

### Pre-sampling

- Announce your intention to sample as a [nanopublication](https://nanodash.knowledgepixels.com/publish?template=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fnp%2FRAX1sVMU6z8BS41dsoXFktK6K5Vw0QgZ4vmAEWANhFiqs%2Ftemplate&template-version=latest&context=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fspaces%2Fknowledgepixels%2Fnanodash%2Fr%2Fhome)

### Sampling

- In the sampling location, determine decimal coordinates, altitude, and temperature. Take note of the sampling date and time.
- Take a picture of sampling site and/or register the biome. Take note of the type of soil sample you collect.
- Collect a soil sample of approximately 50 grams from the sampling location. Sampling depth should be approximately 10 cm.
- To prevent accidental mix-ups, label the sample tube with a unique identifier such as a [UUID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universally_unique_identifier)

### Sample processing

Soil samples should be dried as soon as possible to prevent decomposition and/or formation of molds.

- Dry the sample in a dark place at appproximately 25-30 °C for 2-3 days until the sample is visibly dried. 
- Declare the sample collection as a [nanopublication](https://nanodash.knowledgepixels.com/publish?template=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fnp%2FRAEJRjmoJEBDAP6DoW24bKA2xCsk2V-t4395Uo4TmTGUs%2Ftemplate&template-version=latest&context=https%3A%2F%2Fw3id.org%2Fspaces%2Fknowledgepixels%2Fnanodash%2Fr%2Fhome)
- Store the sample at room temperature.

## Outcomes

- A soil sample with associated metadata.
- A nanopublication describing the sample.

## Notes

- Soil samples can be dried on a clean A4 paper sheet. Once dry, they can be returned into the original sample collection tube and stored.
