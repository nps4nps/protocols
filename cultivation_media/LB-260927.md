Protocol
=======

### Reagents


| Name          | CAS-Number | Amount |
|---------------|--------|--------|
| Tryptone      | 91079-40-2 | 10 G/L |
| NaCl      | 7647-14-5 | 10 G/L |
| Yeast extract | 8013-01-02 | 5 G/L  |

### Steps

- Mix components with demineralized water
- Autoclave using a program with 121 °C for 20 mins.


### Notes

- For a solid version, 20 G/L or 7 G/L Agar-Agar, Kobe I (CAS: 9002-18-0) can be added for hard and soft agar, respectively.
