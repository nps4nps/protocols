Protocol
=======

### Reagents


| Name          | CAS-Number | Amount |
|---------------|--------|--------|
| Dextrose      | 50-99-7 | 10 G/L |
| Soluble starch | 65996-62-5 | 20 G/L  | 
| Tryptone      | 91079-40-2 | 4 G/L |
| Yeast extract | 8013-01-02 | 2 G/L  |
| Meat extract | 68990-09-0 | 2 G/L  |
| CaCO3         | 471-34-1 | 3 G/L  |

### Steps

- Mix components with demineralized water
- Adjust pH to 7.2 before autoclaving
- Autoclave using a program with 121 °C for 15 mins.


### Notes

- pH after autoclaving ~ 6.9
